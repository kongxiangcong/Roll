# Operating cashflows and controlled economic review (05)

`CompanyResearch.calculate_operating_valuation(OperatingValuationCommand)` and `POST /api/company-research/operating-valuations` build a frozen operating model, a separate immutable review and a child shared snapshot. A model candidate cannot set review status. The original03 snapshot and04 diagnostic input/result remain unchanged. The child copies the frozen company/evidence/hypotheses/core research and pins `valuation_ids`, `economic_review_ids` and the model ID; DAILY/REPORT must use those IDs, never select an independent latest valuation.

The command extends `ValuationCommand` with `drivers` and `economic_basis`. Omit `config.scenarios`: the host constructs full FCFF paths. Existing04 source, numerical-origin, scale, currency, cutoff, bridge and replay checks are retained.

## Operating model

`drivers={scenarios,normalizations,provenance}` contains1–10 named scenarios, each with `base_revenue` and1–50 consecutive complete calendar-year rows. A row contains `period=YYYY-01-01/YYYY-12-31`, either `revenue_growth` or `revenue_driver={volume_growth,price_growth}`, plus `operating_margin,tax_rate,depreciation,maintenance_capex,growth_capex,delta_working_capital,invested_capital`. Revenue is prior revenue times its growth factor, or prior revenue times both volume and price factors. The model never divides group earnings by partial product output. An explicit aggregate revenue-growth assumption remains available where a same-scope volume/price series is unavailable.

EBIT is revenue times operating margin. Cash-tax proxy is positive EBIT times tax rate; early losses receive no automatic cash refund. NOPAT is EBIT minus that tax. Net reinvestment is maintenance capex + growth capex + change in working capital − depreciation. FCFF is NOPAT minus net reinvestment. Negative early FCFF is allowed; the04 terminal-positive-base rule still applies. Negative revenue, depreciation/capex, nonpositive invested capital, growth≤−100%, tax outside0–100%, margin>100% or nonfinite arithmetic are refused.

Version1 explicitly supports stable capital turnover: the first forecast inherits unchanged baseline revenue scale; investment at year-end t funds opening capital in t+1. The host checks next opening capital = prior opening capital + prior net reinvestment and stable revenue/opening-capital turnover. A price increase, idle-capacity utilization, asset disposal or changing turnover requires a separately supported path; it cannot be silently treated as reinvestment-driven growth. The host does not claim current-year sales growth universally equals current-year reinvestment/opening capital.

At steady state, final-year ROIC must equal NOPAT/opening capital and declared terminal ROIC. Final net-reinvestment/NOPAT must equal g/ROIC, final revenue growth must equal g, and maintenance/lease-renewal reasoning must be explicit. These conditions make year-end reinvestment support next-year terminal growth; the terminal base already includes reinvestment. Different scenarios may specify a `terminal.roic` map. This is one supported steady-state model, not independent evidence that terminal assumptions will occur.

Every numeric driver/WACC constituent has exact-path `{kind,references,unit,currency,scope,period}` provenance. DISCLOSED/DERIVED must match a frozen fact's kind/value/basis; DERIVED needs its saved derivation. ASSUMED cites explicit frozen-anchored assumptions. Forecast FCFF is saved as a separate host DERIVED record with company, value, units, period, formula, exact input/provenance hash, transitive assumptions and normalization hash.04 receives an explicitly ASSUMED forecast linked to that model and derived-record hash; a future assumption is never relabeled a historical disclosure.

Each normalization saves `adjustment_id,target,before,delta,after,formula,reason,references,provenance`. The supported formula is exactly `before + delta`; each scalar target and adjustment ID may occur once. The original value and all three numeric origins are retained. Invalid/duplicated adjustments block review and are not silently compounded into diagnostic inputs.

## Economic basis and four states

`economic_basis` contains `applicability,wacc,terminal,bridge,limitations` and `assumption_basis,valuation_basis` (omission saves only a blocked diagnostic).

- `applicability={category,reason,references}`: NON_FINANCIAL, CYCLICAL, GROUP, CYCLICAL_GROUP or FINANCIAL. Frozen business/scope facts conservatively identify disclosed commodity and industrial/trading/financial/associate boundaries; ordinary financial-instrument notes or the words “group” do not themselves create a financial-business classification. Safety context is retained from immutable ancestors, so omitting a known scope fact in a later freeze cannot remove the guard. A cyclical requires sourced peak/trough, a multi-year full-cycle rationale and actually applied adjustments; two-year averaging is insufficient. A complex group requires sourced segment revenue/profit/capital sums reconciled with consolidated scope and a financial/associate policy. Financial companies retain a NOT_APPLICABLE method/review and research snapshot; the FCFF executor is not called and valuation IDs remain empty.
- `wacc` saves risk-free rate, beta, equity risk premium, pretax debt cost, equity/debt weights, tax rate, currency, date, tax-shield reason and fact references. The host checks `(rf + beta*ERP)*wE + costDebt*(1-tax)*wD`, nonnegative constituents/weights, weights summing to1 and model currency/date. Risk-free and any nonzero-debt cost/tax rate require matching dated numeric facts. Beta/ERP or capitalization proxies must have their own sourced economic rationale. The source's total ERP must not be counted a second time as country premium.
- `bridge` saves `shares_reason,references,components,exclusions,overlap_reason`. Each signed calculator row is matched by component ID/value to a category and unique economic ID; entries already included in FCFF cannot be added again. DEBT, CASH, RESTRICTED_CASH, NCI, NONOPERATING_ASSETS and ASSOCIATES are each covered by a component or explicit sourced exclusion. Restricted cash receives no free-cash addback. Dilution, repurchase liabilities, leases and capital-project payables require consistent one-time treatment. WACC interest-bearing debt may legitimately differ from bridge obligations; that difference must be explicit.
- `assumption_basis` is keyed by `config/<numeric path>` and `drivers/<numeric path>`. Each record has `reason,support_fact_version_ids,counter_fact_version_ids,limitations`. Missing rationale or unrelated/non-numeric evidence blocks review. Source-role checks require relevant economic metrics, not a business-description citation for a rate, share count or cash bridge. Explicitly supported proxies/stress conditions with known limitations are conditional. These checks establish reproducible source/basis and model consistency; they are not an independent audit of issuer statements or empirical validation of forecasts.

`unreviewed` is04's immutable diagnostic state.05's host produces `blocked` on any required failed check, `conditional` when every required check is supported but explicit constraints remain, and `reviewed` only when all applicable checks, including sourced assumption audits, pass with no remaining recorded limitation. ASSUMED numbers do not automatically forbid reviewed: a supported research assumption can be economically checked. A caller/model cannot request a state or upgrade itself merely by declaring confidence. Reviewed does not validate returns or grant strategy authority. Every model freezes the actual economics source archive; every review pins its policy archive/version (`economic-review-2`). Legacy policy1 results remain immutable diagnostics and are hidden by the safe presentation layer.

## Dates, safe presentation and recovery

`valuation_basis={valuation_basis_date,financial_period_end,bridge_as_of,latest_known_financial_period_end,current_pricing_gaps}` separates value anchor from knowledge cutoff. Version1 requires a December31 anchor and a complete next-calendar-year first cashflow; no unsupported stub is inserted or omitted. `config.metadata.as_of` remains the frozen calculation/quote cutoff, while source observation/receipt/consumption dates retain their own meanings. A current acquisition of older reports never becomes historical PIT.

Use `valuation_presentation(review_id,purpose='CURRENT_PRICING'|'BASELINE_RESEARCH')` or `GET /economic-reviews/{id}/presentation`. The default current-pricing view evaluates actual read time (`evaluated_at`) independently of the value anchor. An unknown or>180-day-old cash bridge, a newer known financial period or explicit current gap hides scenarios/prices/ranges. New same-company financial disclosures acquired after the snapshot also block current use and return their version IDs; they are not mixed into saved cashflows or historical reports. `BASELINE_RESEARCH` can expose a supported dated conditional calculation with all assumptions/limits while keeping current use blocked. This purpose cannot override a blocked economic review.

`GET /operating-models/{id}` and `GET /economic-reviews/{id}` expose frozen audit details. `GET /valuations/{id}/presentation` exposes an unreviewed diagnostic without prices; it does not search for a newer review. Raw04 values remain available only as explicitly diagnostic detail/replay. Future quick summaries/reports must consume the safe pinned-review view.

Models, reviewed views, source originals, policy source and child snapshots use existing SQLite/EvidenceStore canonical archives and Backups. Recovery checks company/snapshot/input/model/review/valuation bindings, policy/source hashes and immutable core references.04 offline replay still recomputes its saved arithmetic without network/model calls. No ForecastBundle, account event, Decision, CapabilityGrant, ExecutionIntent, old budget or SEALED data is changed.

06 adds a necessary `operating_scope` consistency guard: v1 consolidated FCFF requires consolidated operating driver origins and relevant consolidated source/derived facts for assumptions. A partial segment margin cannot be multiplied by group revenue merely by labeling the forecast consolidated. New reviews block that mismatch; read-time safe presentation also blocks incompatible older immutable models for both purposes. Old review/source records stay unchanged. See [comparison semantics and evidence](company-change-api.md).
