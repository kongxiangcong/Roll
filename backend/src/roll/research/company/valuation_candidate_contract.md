# Operating cashflows and controlled economic review (05)

`CompanyResearch.calculate_operating_valuation(OperatingValuationCommand)` and `POST /api/company-research/operating-valuations` build a frozen operating model, a separate immutable review and a child shared snapshot. A model candidate cannot set review status. The original03 snapshot and04 diagnostic input/result remain unchanged. The child copies the frozen company/evidence/hypotheses/core research and pins `valuation_ids`, `economic_review_ids` and the model ID; DAILY/REPORT must use those IDs, never select an independent latest valuation.

The command extends `ValuationCommand` with `drivers` and `economic_basis`. Omit `config.scenarios`: the host constructs full FCFF paths. Existing04 source, numerical-origin, scale, currency, cutoff, bridge and replay checks are retained.

## Operating model

`drivers={scenarios,normalizations,provenance}` contains1–10 named scenarios, each with `base_revenue` and1–50 consecutive complete calendar-year rows. A row contains `period=YYYY-01-01/YYYY-12-31`, either `revenue_growth` or `revenue_driver={volume_growth,price_growth}`, plus `operating_margin,tax_rate,depreciation,maintenance_capex,growth_capex,delta_working_capital,invested_capital`. Revenue is prior revenue times its growth factor, or prior revenue times both volume and price factors. The model never divides group earnings by partial product output. An explicit aggregate revenue-growth assumption remains available where a same-scope volume/price series is unavailable.

EBIT is revenue times operating margin. Cash-tax proxy is positive EBIT times tax rate; early losses receive no automatic cash refund. NOPAT is EBIT minus that tax. Net reinvestment is maintenance capex + growth capex + change in working capital − depreciation. FCFF is NOPAT minus net reinvestment. Negative early FCFF is allowed; the04 terminal-positive-base rule still applies. Negative revenue, depreciation/capex, nonpositive invested capital, growth≤−100%, tax outside0–100%, margin>100% or nonfinite arithmetic are refused.

Version1 explicitly supports stable capital turnover: the first forecast inherits unchanged baseline revenue scale; investment at year-end t funds opening capital in t+1. The host checks next opening capital = prior opening capital + prior net reinvestment and stable revenue/opening-capital turnover. A price increase, idle-capacity utilization, asset disposal or changing turnover requires a separately supported path; it cannot be silently treated as reinvestment-driven growth. The host does not claim current-year sales growth universally equals current-year reinvestment/opening capital.

At steady state, final-year ROIC must equal NOPAT/opening capital and declared terminal ROIC. Final net-reinvestment/NOPAT must equal g/ROIC, final revenue growth must equal g, and maintenance/lease-renewal reasoning must be explicit. These conditions make year-end reinvestment support next-year terminal growth; the terminal base already includes reinvestment. Different scenarios may specify a `terminal.roic` map. This is one supported steady-state model, not independent evidence that terminal assumptions will occur.

Every numeric driver/WACC constituent has exact-path `{kind,references,unit,currency,scope,period}` provenance. DISCLOSED/DERIVED must match a frozen fact's kind/value/basis; DERIVED needs its saved derivation. ASSUMED cites explicit frozen-anchored assumptions. Forecast FCFF is saved as a separate host DERIVED record with company, value, units, period, formula, exact input/provenance hash, transitive assumptions and normalization hash.04 receives an explicitly ASSUMED forecast linked to that model and derived-record hash; a future assumption is never relabeled a historical disclosure.

Each normalization saves `adjustment_id,target,before,delta,after,formula,reason,references,provenance`. The supported formula is exactly `before + delta`; each scalar target and adjustment ID may occur once. The original value and all three numeric origins are retained. Invalid/duplicated adjustments block review and are not silently compounded into diagnostic inputs.

## Economic basis and four states

`economic_basis` contains `applicability,wacc,terminal,bridge,limitations` and `assumption_basis,valuation_basis` (omission saves only a blocked diagnostic).

- `applicability={category,reason,references}`: NON_FINANCIAL, CYCLICAL, GROUP, CYCLICAL_GROUP or FINANCIAL. Frozen business/scope facts conservatively identify disclosed commodity and industrial/trading/financial/associate boundaries; ordinary financial-instrument notes or the words “group” do not themselves create a financial-business classification. Safety context is retained from immutable ancestors, so omitting a known scope fact in a later freeze cannot remove the guard. A cyclical requires sourced peak/trough, a multi-year full-cycle rationale and actually applied adjustments; two-year averaging is insufficient. A complex group requires sourced segment revenue/profit/capital sums reconciled with consolidated scope and a financial/associate policy. Financial companies retain a NOT_APPLICABLE method/review and research snapshot; the FCFF executor is not called and valuation IDs remain empty.
- `wacc` saves risk-free rate, beta, equity risk premium, pretax debt cost, equity/debt weights, tax rate, currency, date, tax-shield reason and fact references. The host checks `(rf + beta*ERP)*wE + costDebt*(1-tax)*wD`, nonnegative constituents/weights, weights summing to1 and model currency/date. Risk-free and any nonzero-debt cost/tax rate require matching dated numeric facts. Beta/ERP or capitalization proxies must have their own sourced economic rationale. The source's total ERP must not be counted a second time as country premium.
- `bridge` saves `shares_reason,references,components,exclusions,overlap_reason`. Each signed calculator row is matched by component ID/value to a category and unique economic ID; entries already included in FCFF cannot be added again. DEBT, CASH, RESTRICTED_CASH, NCI, NONOPERATING_ASSETS and ASSOCIATES are each covered by a component or explicit sourced exclusion. Restricted cash receives no free-cash addback. Dilution, repurchase liabilities, leases and capital-project payables require consistent one-time treatment. WACC interest-bearing debt may legitimately differ from bridge obligations; that difference must be explicit.
- `assumption_basis` is keyed by `config/<numeric path>` and `drivers/<numeric path>`. Each record has `reason,support_fact_version_ids,counter_fact_version_ids,limitations`. Missing rationale or unrelated/non-numeric evidence blocks review. Source-role checks require relevant economic metrics, not a business-description citation for a rate, share count or cash bridge. Explicitly supported proxies/stress conditions with known limitations are conditional. These checks establish reproducible source/basis and model consistency; they are not an independent audit of issuer statements or empirical validation of forecasts.

`unreviewed` is04's immutable diagnostic state.05's host produces `blocked` on any required failed check, `conditional` when every required check is supported but explicit constraints remain, and `reviewed` only when all applicable checks, including sourced assumption audits, pass with no remaining recorded limitation. ASSUMED numbers do not automatically forbid reviewed: a supported research assumption can be economically checked. A caller/model cannot request a state or upgrade itself merely by declaring confidence. Reviewed does not validate returns or grant strategy authority. Every model freezes the actual economics source archive; every review pins its policy archive/version (`economic-review-2`). Legacy policy1 results remain immutable diagnostics and are hidden by the safe presentation layer.

## Dates, safe presentation and recovery

`valuation_basis={valuation_basis_date,financial_period_end,bridge_as_of,latest_known_financial_period_end,current_pricing_gaps}` separates value anchor from knowledge cutoff. Version1 requires a December31 anchor and a complete next-calendar-year first cashflow; no unsupported stub is inserted or omitted. `config.metadata.as_of` remains the frozen calculation/quote cutoff, while source observation/receipt/consumption dates retain their own meanings. A current acquisition of older reports never becomes historical PIT.

Use `valuation_presentation(review_id,purpose='CURRENT_PRICING'|'BASELINE_RESEARCH')` or `GET /economic-reviews/{id}/presentation`. The default current-pricing view evaluates actual read time (`evaluated_at`) independently of the value anchor. An unknown or>180-day-old cash bridge, a newer known financial period or explicit current gap hides scenarios/prices/ranges. New same-company financial disclosures acquired after the snapshot also block current use and return their version IDs; they are not mixed into saved cashflows or historical reports. `BASELINE_RESEARCH` can expose a supported dated conditional calculation with all assumptions/limits while keeping current use blocked. This purpose cannot override a blocked economic review.

`GET /operating-models/{id}` and `GET /economic-reviews/{id}` expose frozen audit details. `GET /valuations/{id}/presentation` exposes an unreviewed diagnostic without prices; it does not search for a newer review. Raw04 values remain available only as explicitly diagnostic detail/replay. Future quick summaries/reports must consume the safe pinned-review view.

Models, reviewed views, source originals, policy source and child snapshots use existing SQLite/EvidenceStore canonical archives and Backups. Recovery checks company/snapshot/input/model/review/valuation bindings, policy/source hashes and immutable core references.04 offline replay still recomputes its saved arithmetic without network/model calls. No ForecastBundle, account event, Decision, CapabilityGrant, ExecutionIntent, old budget or SEALED data is changed.

06 adds a necessary `operating_scope` consistency guard: v1 consolidated FCFF requires consolidated operating driver origins and relevant consolidated source/derived facts for assumptions. A partial segment margin cannot be multiplied by group revenue merely by labeling the forecast consolidated. New reviews block that mismatch; read-time safe presentation also blocks incompatible older immutable models for both purposes. Old review/source records stay unchanged. See [comparison semantics and evidence](company-change-api.md).
# Company valuation calculator and immutable diagnostic versions

Ticket04 adds `CompanyResearch.calculate_valuation(ValuationCommand)` at `/api/company-research/valuations`. It never writes account valuation, forecasts, permissions, decisions or execution intents. Every saved output is **unreviewed**, `usable_range=false`. Unsupported economic assumptions remain diagnostic until ticket05 provides operating drivers, normalization and controlled economic review.

Ticket05 now supplies a [separate operating model, economic review and safe presentation](company-economic-review.md). It does not mutate04 inputs/results or relax their numeric-origin checks. Summaries use a pinned review ID and explicit presentation purpose; raw calculator prices remain diagnostics.

## Public boundary

- `POST /valuations`: `snapshot_id`, `config`, `assumptions`, `provenance`, optional `parent_version_id`, `change_reason`, `idempotency_key`.
- `GET /valuation-inputs/{version_id}` and `GET /valuations/{valuation_id}`: immutable records, canonical archive validation.
- `GET /valuations/{valuation_id}/bundle`: standalone JSON containing frozen input/result, snapshot/evidence packet, original materials, calculator sources and full licenses. Material originals are bounded to 36 MiB; larger packets use the full backup path.
- `POST /valuations/{valuation_id}/replay`: deterministic replay with no network/model calls. The standalone equivalent is `python -m roll.research.company.valuation_replay --bundle file.json` with the matching Roll method version installed; no SQLite store or external developer checkout is needed.

Replay checks archived source hashes against the exact installed version before calculating. It refuses `CALCULATOR_VERSION_UNAVAILABLE` on source drift, retaining the complete frozen sources for reinstall/recovery. It does not execute code supplied in a bundle. Input changes append a new version and parent reference; published 03 snapshots stay unchanged. The saved valuation links back to snapshot ID/hash, frozen company identity, facts and hypothesis versions.

## Calculator config

`roll.research.company.calculator.calculate(config, frozen_company)` is the deterministic arithmetic seam; the service additionally verifies reference origins and snapshot binding. The licensed `valuation_reference` module is compatibility code, not the controlled publication/review entry.

The config retains upstream FCFF/WACC fields: metadata (`company/as_of/currency/amount_unit/shares_unit/sources/assumptions`), positive `shares`, mutually exclusive `net_debt` or `equity_bridge`, `wacc`, `terminal_g`, and full same-horizon `scenarios`. Each scenario has unique `name` and either annual `fcf`, or annual `revenue` plus `fcf_margin`. Amount/share pairs are `unit/share`, `million/million_shares`, `100_million/100_million_shares`. Monetary values use one model currency; shares have no currency. Rates and margins are ratios.

Scenario weights are optional: omit `probability` for every scenario to omit the weighted price entirely. If supplied for all scenarios, finite nonnegative weights must sum to one and are explicitly researcher assumptions, never event/upside probabilities. No weights are synthesized. The diagnostic scenario range is not a confidence interval or usable price range.

Equity bridge rows retain `name`, signed `amount`, `source`, `treatment` and optional stable `component_id`. Positive deductions, negative additions and zero exclusions preserve `EV - sum(bridge) = parent equity`. Duplicate normalized names or component IDs and contradictory signs are rejected. Restricted cash can remain explicitly excluded (zero adjustment with source/treatment); NCI, associates and nonoperating assets remain individually visible. Whether different named rows economically overlap belongs to ticket05, rather than being inferred from arithmetic.

DCF uses year-end discounting and an explicitly positive final base FCFF, then year N+1 terminal FCFF. Early negative FCFF is valid. Nonfinite numbers, mixed scales and WACC <= g are rejected. Sensitivity accepts `{scenario,wacc,terminal_g}` and saves each valid cell or its rejection; invalid cells do not remove the base calculation. Limits: 10 scenarios, 50 annual periods, 400 sensitivity cells, 128 KiB service request.

EPV additionally requires `{normalized_fcff,applicability:"APPLICABLE",normalization_basis}`; absence of the declaration/basis returns NOT_APPLICABLE. Its input declaration remains unreviewed and must describe maintenance reinvestment normalization; it is never labeled independent validation of DCF. `perpetuity(100,.1)=1000` and `perpetuity(100,.08)=1250` are separate closed-form arithmetic examples, not finite forecast inputs.

## Provenance

Each scalar numeric leaf in `config` has an exact slash path in `provenance`, e.g. `scenarios/0/fcf/0`. Its record is `{kind,references,unit,currency,scope,period}`. Monetary `unit` matches the model amount scale; shares use its share scale with null currency; ratios use `ratio` and null currency; class prices use `price_per_share` and the actual listing currency. Periods/scope cannot be empty.

`ASSUMED` values cite an explicit assumption record (`assumption_id`, `statement`, `anchor_fact_version_ids`, `hypothesis_version_ids`) whose anchors are frozen in this snapshot. This stores the forecast assumption and its limits instead of bare future FCFF. `DISCLOSED` and `DERIVED` cannot cite assumptions or hypotheses as numeric facts: exactly one frozen fact of the corresponding kind must match value/unit/currency/scope and `period_start/period_end`; DERIVED also needs its saved derivation. No implicit unit/currency conversion is performed. Any required conversion must first become an explicit derived fact with its formula and references. Ticket05 supplies the quantitative operating bridge and economic checks; current acceptance of a cited assumption does not endorse its economics. WACC construction and long-run reinvestment reasoning can be retained in explicit assumption statements; missing structured economic checks remain an acknowledged gap until ticket05.

## Market basis and reverse calculation

`reverse` accepts `scenario`, `market_basis` and optionally paired `steady_margins/base_revenue` plus `comparable_margin_basis`. It never accepts a caller aggregate market value or one price times all shares. The frozen company identity determines all required listings, regardless of client `metadata.listing_structure`.

Every class row contains `listing_id`, `shares`, `shares_unit`, `shares_as_of`, positive `price`, `currency`, aware `priced_at`, `share_refs`, `price_refs`; a different currency requires direct `fx:{rate,from_currency,to_currency,as_of,source_refs}`. Counts sum to model shares. All quotes and share/FX dates are the same Shanghai trading day; quotes cannot exceed the exact model cutoff, itself no later than the snapshot. Date-only cutoff means end of the Shanghai date. Same-currency FX is absent or exactly one. The service requires these values to match frozen numeric facts: share/price scope is the listing ID, FX scope is `FROM->TO`, and period is that single day. Their references cannot be replaced by assumed prices.

Missing A/H class, price, FX or incompatible date/units refuses the reverse component while retaining the base diagnostic. Reference corruption is rejected at publication. Required year N+1 FCFF holds all years 1..N fixed. Nonpositive requirements have explicit `no_positive_terminal_requirement` status and empty revenue rows. Revenue conversion requires an explicit same-scope steady FCFF-margin basis and uses N+1 periods for CAGR; it does not identify the market's unique growth assumption or causal explanation.

## Automatic report candidate wire (version 1)

The response envelope is `{snapshot_id,input_sha256,command_json,gaps}`. `command_json` is null for unsupported inputs; otherwise it encodes only `{config,assumptions,provenance,change_reason,drivers,economic_basis}`. Snapshot, idempotency and parent binding are host owned. Never include `review`, `status`, precomputed FCFF or result prices. Every number remains a value plus exact-path origin, not prose.

Additional exact field names for the existing economic contract:

- `economic_basis.wacc={risk_free,beta,equity_risk_premium,cost_of_debt,equity_weight,debt_weight,tax_rate,currency,as_of,tax_shield_reason,references}`.
- `economic_basis.terminal={roic,maintenance_reason,references}`; roic may be a map keyed by scenario name. A numeric leaf in such a map has its own provenance path (for example `basis/terminal/roic/base`).
- `economic_basis.bridge={shares_reason,references,components,exclusions,overlap_reason}`. A component has `{component_id,economic_id,included_in_fcff,category,amount,reason,references}`; an exclusion has `{category,reason,references}`. No unknown balance is an exclusion. A signed cash bridge amount needs explicit derivation/sign treatment and an anchor to the actual reported cash balance. Automatic bridge/share origins also require numeric source facts at the declared bridge date.
- `economic_basis.valuation_basis={valuation_basis_date,financial_period_end,bridge_as_of,latest_known_financial_period_end,current_pricing_gaps}`; financial dates refer to the actual facts. Do not date an assumed roll-forward as an observed balance. `config.metadata.as_of` must equal the exact snapshot.as_of timestamp; wacc.as_of uses its date portion. Historical-year-end valuation_basis_date is separate.
- `assumptions` rows are exactly `{assumption_id,statement,anchor_fact_version_ids,hypothesis_version_ids}`. `economic_basis.assumption_basis` maps every ASSUMED input's `config/<path>` or `drivers/<path>` to `{reason,support_fact_version_ids,counter_fact_version_ids,limitations}`.
- `drivers.provenance` includes numeric leaves in `drivers.scenarios` (paths `scenarios/...`) and `economic_basis.wacc/terminal` (paths `basis/wacc/...` and `basis/terminal/...`). It does not prefix these paths with `drivers/`. The separate assumption_basis does prefix them with `drivers/`.
- `config.provenance` is not a config field: `provenance` is a top-level sibling and covers every config numeric leaf. Do not include `config.scenarios`; host computes FCFF.

Construct genuinely different supported operating scenarios. Include WACC/terminal-growth sensitivity in config with provenance when supported. Unsupported reverse DCF/EPV or sensitivity must be named as gaps rather than fabricated supporting evidence. Scenario weights are optional research weights and never market probabilities. All automated candidate models retain a host-imposed conditional-verification limitation even if the candidate removes its own limitations. Current-purpose rejection never changes the requested purpose to baseline.
