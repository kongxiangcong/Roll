## Comparator fidelity

A regime already above the mean creates no entry; an equality-to-positive crossing creates one entry, and remaining above does not automatically reenter after exit. ## Conditional subset intervals

The original full-population interval remains date-mean based. Conditional selected/bin calculations now jointly resample each date's member score sum and count with the registered consecutive calendar blocks (20/40), seed1701 and2000 replicates. Observed nonmembership contributes zero weight while retaining its calendar position. Conditional ratio equals total resampled member scores divided by total resampled member count. Truly absent checkpoints, unknown labels, unmatured labels and unknown same-day members remain explicit missing inputs and cannot become zero outcomes. Empty subsets or a zero resample denominator remain insufficient, with absent endpoints; they are neither discarded nor retried until a favorable resample appears.

Member support is conservatively reported from occupied **date** count divided by block length, not total security count or all nonmember dates. A count is still an upper bound, never a stationarity/independence proof. Logical duplicate events do not add member counts or support. New `20-statistical-method-v2` freezes the conditional algorithm statement and pre-result80-slot alternating-membership arithmetic checks. All real assumptions,30-block eligibility, selected/bin minimum cases, thresholds, intersection-union policy and final PASS-look count remain unchanged.
